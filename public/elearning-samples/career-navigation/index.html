    Plan for who is in the room

    The same pathway lands differently with different learners.

    Opportunity youth

    Ages 16 to 24, not in school or working

    • Short, job-linked programs
    • Clear structure
    • Foundational digital skills

    Adults without a four-year degree

    Skills built on the job, in the military or at community college

    • Credit for prior learning
    • Skill validation
    • Fast tracks

    Returning citizens

    Reentering the community after incarceration

    • Confidence building
    • Device and internet access
