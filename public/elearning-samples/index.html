Sample 1 · Self-paced course module

Career Navigation in a Digital Economy

A six-lesson online module built for the career navigation staff of a regional workforce organization. Learners work with local labor market data, digital credentials, job search tools, AI and program dashboards, then finish with a written action plan.

Audience
Adult education and workforce staff who coach job seekers
Format
Fully online and self-paced, about 70 minutes across six lessons
Open sample 1(opens in a new tab)

Instructional design features

  • Branching scenarios

    Learners coach a job seeker through a conversation and see how each choice plays out.

  • Knowledge checks with retry

    Each check explains why an answer is right or wrong and lets the learner try again.

  • Hands-on practice tools

    A research tool, an AI prompt builder and a data dashboard let learners try each task themselves.

  • One section at a time

    Content opens in short sections as the learner moves forward, so no screen is overloaded.

  • Saved progress

    The course remembers where each learner stopped and which lessons are done.

  • Personal action plan

    The last lesson turns the learner's choices into a plan they can copy and use at work.

  • Accessible by design

    Works with a keyboard and a screen reader, meets WCAG AA color contrast, and fits phone screens.

Sample 2 · Peer learning platform

CLP Academy

A working preview of the platform for a CLP cohort academy. It joins live sessions with self-paced work, so learners study, build a project and get feedback from peers and a coach in one place.

Audience
Educators and program leaders learning together in a cohort
Format
Blended: weekly live sessions plus self-paced work between them
Open sample 2(opens in a new tab)

Instructional design features

  • Guided walkthroughs

    Step-by-step demos, a looping screen demo and a chaptered recording show each tool in use.

  • Articles with peer highlights

    Learners mark key passages and see what classmates marked.

  • Project gallery

    Learners publish projects, post updates and comment on each other's work.

  • Cohort chat and polls

    Channels, reactions and live polls keep the group in conversation between sessions.

  • One-on-one coaching

    Learners set goals, book time with a coach and keep a private thread with them.

  • Weekly path and digest

    Each week lists the live session, tasks and project steps, and a digest sums up cohort activity.

Sample 3 · Mobile interactive story

Skills-Based Hiring Micro-Learning

A short interactive story built for SkillRise, an ISTE initiative for adult learning and workforce programs. It shows how people build skills at school, at work, while volunteering and on their own, why many of those skills are hard to show on a resume, and how digital credentials can show verified skills.

Audience
Adult learners and job seekers, and the staff who support them
Format
Mobile-first micro-learning in short story segments
Open sample 3(opens in a new tab)

Instructional design features

  • Character-driven stories

    Learners follow people whose skills came from many parts of life.

  • Learner choices

    Taps and choices move each story forward at the learner's pace.

  • Made for phones

    Short screens and large tap targets suit learning in a few spare minutes.

  • Reflection on your own skills

    Prompts ask learners to name skills from their own work, school and life.

  • Credentials in context

    Digital credentials appear inside the stories, tied to a real job search.
